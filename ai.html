NORTHBRIGDE AI

AI that works from company context.

Northbrigde AI is designed to explain what is happening across the company by reading the records the signed-in user is authorized to access.

Ask operational questions

  • What needs my attention today?
  • Which projects are delayed?
  • What changed since yesterday?
  • Which approvals are waiting for me?
  • Summarize my department this week.

Permission-aware by design

If an analyst cannot access confidential finance records, Northbrigde AI should not reveal those records either. Permission checks happen before information is retrieved for AI.

THE MODEL

AI does not magically know what happened.

Company activity must first enter Northbrigde through employee updates, workflows, documents, integrations or connected systems. Northbrigde records the activity, dashboards show it and AI explains it.

Company activity→Northbrigde records→Permission check→AI answer
