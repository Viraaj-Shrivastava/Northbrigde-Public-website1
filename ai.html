HOW WE APPROACH IT

Assistive by design.

The principles we hold ourselves to when adding AI to an operating system companies depend on.

People approve

Answers are traceable

Output points back to the records it came from, so it can be checked rather than trusted blindly.

Access is never widened

AI inherits the permissions of the person using it. It is not a route around your access rules.
