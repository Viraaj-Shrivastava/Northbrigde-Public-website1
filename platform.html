Designed to scale in detail

From summary to source record.

Northbridge keeps complex organizations navigable through a consistent path: summary, category, list, and individual record. Leaders can start with the whole company while operators can move directly into the work that needs attention.

The same model supports many departments and workflows without forcing every person to see the same interface—or more information than their role permits.
