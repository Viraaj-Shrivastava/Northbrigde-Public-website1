PLATFORM

One system for structure, work and information.

Northbridge brings the moving parts of an organization into a single configured environment—so a task, the document behind it, the approval it needs and the number it moves all live in the same place.

CORE MODULES

What the platform gives you.

Permissions

HOW IT FITS TOGETHER

Configured once, connected throughout.

The organization model is the foundation

Because structure is defined at the platform level, everything downstream inherits it. Add a role and its permissions, queues and dashboard scope follow automatically—no reconfiguring five separate tools.

  • One definition of who reports to whom
  • Permissions derived from role, not assigned by hand
  • Structure changes propagate across the system
